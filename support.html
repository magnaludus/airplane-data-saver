Support

Need help or found a problem?
Open a support request (free GitHub account needed). Please include your macOS version and what you saw. Replies usually come within two business days.

Setting up

The first time you turn Airplane on, macOS asks you to allow its network filter. Open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions, find Network Extensions, and switch on Airplane. Airplane must be in your Applications folder.

An app I need is blocked

Open Airplane from the menu bar. Recently blocked apps are listed at the top; click Allow. You can also add apps in Settings.

My internet stopped working

Choose Off in the Airplane menu, or quit Airplane. Airplane is built to let traffic through whenever it can't do its job, so quitting always restores normal access. If it still doesn't work, please open a support request.

Little Snitch, VPNs and other filters

Airplane works alongside other network filters and VPNs, but two filters both blocking can be confusing. Airplane tells you when it sees another filter. If an app is blocked while Airplane is Off, check your other filter.

What Airplane can't block

macOS sends a small amount of its own traffic in a way no third-party filter can see. Airplane's flight presets stop the big background syncs it can see, but it can't promise that nothing at all leaves your Mac.

Removing Airplane

In Airplane's Settings, choose Remove Completely…. This removes the network filter and everything Airplane stored. Then move the app to the Trash.
