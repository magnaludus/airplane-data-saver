Airplane: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 7, 2026. Publisher: Joseph Peterson.

Airplane collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you or your Mac to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

  • The list of apps you allow, your On / Off / Auto choice, and the names of networks you marked as metered. These are stored only on your Mac.
  • Traffic counts shown in the menu (how much each app used) are kept in memory and shown only to you. They are not written to disk and are not sent anywhere.

How it works

Airplane installs a network filter (a macOS system extension) that decides, on your Mac, which apps may use the network. The filter looks at which app is opening a connection so it can allow or block it. It does not read the content of your traffic and does not report it.

Removing your data

In Airplane's settings, choose Remove Completely… before deleting the app. It removes the network filter and everything Airplane stored.

Changes and contact
